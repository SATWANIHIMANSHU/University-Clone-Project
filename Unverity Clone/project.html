WHEN YOU ARE AT GUNI, YOU ARE
Be it campus placements across the A to Z of the corporate world - from Alembic to HDFC and from Infosys to Zydus, pursuing further studies in some of the world's premier educational institutes, founding a start-up or helping grow an existing family business, Ganpat University ensures its students are perfectly placed to pursue their ambition by imparting education that goes beyond just the books.

A thoughtfully designed and carefully nurtured vibrant campus culture ensures that students turn into well-rounded and sought-after professionals, while experiencing some of the best times of their lives. Imbibing perspectives from not only professors but also peers, and learning not only from the regularly updated curriculum but also through the diverse range of ongoing extracurricular activities, Ganpat graduates are well placed to navigate life.
