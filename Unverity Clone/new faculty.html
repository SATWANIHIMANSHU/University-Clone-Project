  • Faculty of Pharmacy at Ganpat University is one of the most prestigious and excellent pharmacy colleges of the nation. Widely known as Shree S. K. Patel College of Pharmaceutical Education and Research (SKPCPER) it was established in 1996 as the first self-financed pharmacy college in North Gujarat region, and the second in the state of Gujarat with a view to imparting Pharmacy Education and Research opportunities.

Faculty of Management Studies

  • Faculty of Management Studies is a research driven, industry oriented academic arm of the University. The Faculty has taken pioneering initiatives like offering functional and sectoral specialization within MBA curriculum, offering industry focused and integrated UG and PG Programs in Healthcare, Agriculture and Services domain. It has included Personality Growth Labs and English Communication Labs in the curriculum and networking with foreign universities for giving global content and exposure.

Faculty of Science

  • Established in the year 2006, Faculty of Science started offering courses like M. Sc. in Biotechnology and Microbiology through Mehsana Urban Institute of Sciences. In a gradual progression, new courses in Biotechnology, Microbiology, Physics, Mathematics and Chemistry have been added while offering undergraduate (including dual degree) and postgraduate programs. FOS is well equipped with ultramodern laboratories and with latest equipments to facilitate the students in their learning in practical way.
  • Faculty of Social Sciences & Humanities (FSSH) at Ganpat University provides the set of skills for a broad range of professional fields in various disciplines viz. Economics, English Literature, Linguistics, Commerce, Social Work, Education, etc. With its varied range of teaching expertise and research orientation, the FSSH promotes interdisciplinary opportunities in social sciences and humanities, offering projects and research work along with the studies.

Faculty of Architecture, Design & Planning

  • Faculty of Architecture, Design and Planning(FADP) aims to provide for its students the finest Architectural,Desigin and Planing education which promotes and expands their creative capacities and sensibilities and establishes the foundation for a creative professional life. FADP is also committed to the belief that one of society’s prime responsibilities is toward learning and education in the deepest sense : To nurture one’s individual creativity and to give freedom of idea exploration of all students is a serious educational responsibility.
  • Kantaben Kashiram Institute of Agriculture Sciences and Research (GUNI-KKIASR) is one of the most ambitious educational initiatives of Ganpat University and is situated Ganpat Vidhyanagar Campus, Kherva, Mehsana, Gujarat. The city is famous for its remarkable cultural and social life.The college is named, Kantaben Kashiram institute of Agriculture Sciences and Research given for generous support of Kantaben Kashiram Patel. The establishment of the Institute is an important landmark in expanding Agricultural education as well as scientific research in Gujarat.

Faculty of Nursing

  • Faculty of Nursing provide a broad-based education for preparation of nursing personnel at both basic and advanced levels who can function in a wide variety of Health Care setting and make their distinctive contribution in all branches of nursing for promotion of health, prevention of diseases, care during illness and maintenance of positive health.To promote the deeper study of nursing with a view to generate new perspectives, and about innovative approaches to deal with problems encountered in nursing and health field.
