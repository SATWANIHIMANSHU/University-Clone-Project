Admission

Education at Ganpat University offers and expects intellectual rigour, deep focus on research, unique opportunities for academic collaboration and creative momentum. As new students, you will join a vibrant, talented community dedicated to academic excellence, personal growth and society’s well-being.

Undergraduate Admission

With a curriculum broad and deep enough to impart specialised knowledge and cross-disciplinary learning, both within and outside the classroom, we equip our students to better navigate the world. The programmes strengthen their base in core subjects while helping them explore and pursue their passions, by creating an engaging and collaborative environment.

Masters Admission

The curriculum of all Master’s programmes is practice based and industry oriented. Our students are sensitised towards contemporary economic and social development. The emphasis is on the development of analytical skills and practical exposure through internships and mentorships. We combine classroom experience with experiential learning through industry-based projects, research and innovation.

Postgraduate Admission

A PhD degree commands great respect and recognition anywhere you go, since it reflects on your commitment & hard work in reaching the pinnacle of academic achievement with significant contribution to knowledge in a specific field/discipline.Through the course of your PhD, you get to build contacts with renowned experts in the field. All these contacts can serve as excellent career references for job positions.

Education Loan

There is no doubt about the importance of education these days. Be it school education or college education, it serves as a stepping stool for you to achieve your dreams. Whether it is college education or post-graduation, don’t let high tuition fees hamper your growth. Getting an education loan is an easy way to finance your dreams. A student loan can help you get into the University of your Choice. The Government’s push on education means you can avail education loan subsidy, and you also get tax benefits for interest paid under Section 80E of the Income Tax Act.

Financial Assistance

The university provides various schemes for financial assistance to the undergraduate, postgraduate,marine, and diploma students in form of scholarships. The other assistance provided to students includes Earn While You Learn scheme, Needy Students’ Fund, Reimbursement of Interest on , Educational Loans, Book Bank facility, and Fellowship. The financial assistance schemes may vary from programme to programme.
